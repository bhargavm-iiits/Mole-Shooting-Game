Modifications (Blender 5.2, headless): arms posed from A-pose to "idle" (arms down) and "cheer" (arms up) and baked;
armature removed; body/head/opacity textures combined into one 1024x1024 atlas (*_atlas.png); decimated from
~7.4k to 2,500 triangles; exported as FBX. Normal/specular maps not used.
